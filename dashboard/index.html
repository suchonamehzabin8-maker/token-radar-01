    How wrong is it? Out-of-sample validation

    Five-fold cross-validation on the 128 live-token protocols: each fold’s multiples were fitted without the protocols it predicts, so every prediction is genuinely out of sample. The model’s typical miss is about 4.6x — implied value ranks protocols, it does not price them.

    How the board is built

    1. Pre-token. A protocol is pre-token when its record carries no token at all: no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol.
    2. Excluded. Wrapped, staked and bridged products of tokens that already trade, CEXs, versioned pools and exchange-named products are never ranked as launches. 198 protocols remain.
    3. Multiple. Each category’s multiple is the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the snapshot’s own live-token protocols in that category, shrunk toward the global median of 0.214x with five phantom protocols, clamped to 0.05x–0.60x.
    4. Growth. Seven-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+100%, moves value by 0.6x its size.
    5. Maturity. A protocol earns from 0.60 at launch to 1.00 after three years without a token; unknown age counts as one year.
    6. Implied value. TVL × multiple × growth × maturity, rounded to the dollar. Rank 1 is the largest.

    Say it plainly: a protocol on this board records no token — that is not proof it has none. And a high implied value is not a prediction that a token is coming, or when. Farm plans are a reader’s checklist, not advice; never pay anyone to claim for you.
